About the shelter

Cozy House is a shelter for homeless animals in Boston. We have been working for more than 10 years and have already found a new home for more than 5000 dogs and cats. We are proud of our work and we are sure that every pet deserves to be loved and cared for.

We are always happy to see new volunteers and we are grateful to everyone who helps us. If you want to help us, please contact us or just come to our shelter. We will be glad to see you!
